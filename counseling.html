Counseling

Talking to someone is a strong, practical step. Here are ways to start.

Ways to get support

Your university

Ask student affairs or the campus counseling office about free or low-cost sessions.

A doctor

A family doctor can talk through how you feel and refer you to a specialist.

Find a doctor in Amman →

Self-guided apps

Woebot is a chatbot that teaches coping skills. It supports you between sessions but does not replace a therapist.

Visit Woebot →

Someone you trust

A friend, family member or mentor can help you feel less alone while you look for professional help.

Before your session

  • Choose a quiet, private place.
  • Check your internet connection if it is online.
  • Jot down what you want to talk about.
  • Be as honest as you can.

Not sure what to say?

Try one of these openers:

  • "I've been feeling overwhelmed lately."
  • "I'm not sleeping well and I don't know why."
  • "I'd like help managing stress."

In a crisis

If you or someone else is in danger, contact your local emergency services immediately and stay with a trusted person.
