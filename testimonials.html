Student Stories

Real pressure, real recovery. Small steps add up.

These stories are illustrative. Names and details are fictional and shared to show that change is possible.

A

Ahmed: from anxiety to excellence

Ahmed struggled with severe anxiety before exams. Through support sessions and daily breathing exercises, he steadily gained control and became one of the top students in his class.

S

Sara: overcoming depression

Sara faced depression during high school. With support from her family and professionals, she began to see the positive side of life, returned to her studies and achieved remarkable results.

L

Leen: from silence to voice

Leen's social anxiety made her avoid taking part. After joining a study club she discovered her abilities and spoke in front of a large audience for the first time.

K

Khaled: breaking the barrier of failure

Khaled failed his first year and nearly gave up. He learned from his mistakes, organised his time and asked classmates for help. The next year he was among the top five in his major.

O

Omar: the weight of expectations

Omar felt immense pressure to excel. With a counselor he learned to manage stress and set realistic goals. Small breaks and a focus on wellbeing improved his health and his focus, and he graduated with honors.
