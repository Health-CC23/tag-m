About Us

We are a group of students from the Applied Science Private University united under the name TAG Masters. We came together with a shared goal: to support and improve the mental health of our fellow students. Realizing the importance of mental well-being in academic and personal success, we built this platform to provide a safe and supportive space for students seeking mental health resources and support.

Our Vision

A university community where every student feels mentally supported, understood, and empowered to thrive.

Our Mission

To offer accessible mental health tools and services tailored to student needs, and to foster a culture of awareness, support, and care within our university.

Our Core Values

  • Confidentiality: Your privacy is always respected.
  • Empathy: We listen and care without judgment.
  • Support: We're here to walk with you, not ahead of you.

Message from Our Team Leader
