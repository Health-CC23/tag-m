About Us

A student team making health support easier to reach.

We are a group of students from Applied Science Private University, united under the name TAG M. We built this platform to give fellow students a safe, simple place to look after their physical and mental health, with free tools, trusted guides and ways to find support.

Our vision

A university community where every student feels healthy, supported and empowered to thrive.

Our mission

To offer accessible health tools and information tailored to student needs, and to build a culture of awareness, support and care.

Our values

Confidentiality

Your privacy is respected. Our tools never save what you enter.

Empathy

We listen and care without judgment.

Support

We walk with you, not ahead of you.

A word from our team leader

"Everyone deserves to feel heard, supported and safe. We believe in standing by each person with compassion and kindness."

TAG M, a student project at Applied Science Private University.
