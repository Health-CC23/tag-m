Health Tools

Calculators and quick references for everyday care. Readings you save stay on your own device.

Dose calculator

Calculates a weight-based dose from the mg/kg value in the prescription or on the label.

For assistance only. Always verify the dose, the drug and the route with the prescriber, the pharmacist or the product label before giving any medicine.

Normal lab values

Typical adult reference ranges. Ranges vary between laboratories, so always use the range printed on the lab report.

Unit converter

Quick conversions used in everyday care.

Teaspoon and tablespoon are approximate. Use a measuring syringe for liquid medicine.

Normal vital signs

Approximate resting ranges by age group.

All ages

Temperature: 36.1 to 37.2 °C (97 to 99 °F). Fever is 38 °C (100.4 °F) or above.
Oxygen saturation (SpO₂): 95 to 100 %. Below 92 % needs urgent medical attention.
Pain score: 0 (none) to 10 (worst). Reassess after every intervention.

Adult blood pressure categories

Blood sugar (adults, mg/dL)

Home care tips

Wound care and injections at home. Follow the instructions of your doctor or nurse first.
