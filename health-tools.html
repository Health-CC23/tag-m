BMI calculator

Body Mass Index compares your weight with your height. It is a rough screening number for adults aged 18 and over.

Enter your height and weight to see your result.

BMI does not measure body fat, muscle, age, sex or ethnicity, and it is not suitable for under 18s or during pregnancy. Treat it as a starting point and talk to a doctor about your health.

Stress check-in

Think about the last two weeks. This is a simple self-reflection, not a diagnosis.

Breathing guide

Slow breathing can help you feel calmer within a few minutes. Pick a pattern, press start and follow the circle.

If you feel dizzy, stop and breathe normally.

Daily water estimate

Enter your weight to get a daily estimate.

A general estimate of total fluid. Needs vary, and some comes from food and other drinks. Follow medical advice if you have a health condition.

Sleep planner

Sleep comes in cycles of about 90 minutes. Waking at the end of a cycle usually feels easier. We allow 15 minutes to fall asleep.

Choose a time to see suggestions.

Most adults do well with 7 to 9 hours. Consistent sleep and wake times matter as much as the total.
